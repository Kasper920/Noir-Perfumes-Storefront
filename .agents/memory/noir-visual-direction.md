name: NOIR theme art direction
description: Keep the photographic hero cinematic in both themes without obscuring its artwork.
---

The hero keeps its dark photographic art direction in both light and dark themes; other site surfaces follow the selected theme.

**Why:** The supplied lineup image has a dark background and needs a readable text gradient, not a replacement light surface. An opaque background color on the gradient overlay completely hides the photograph even when the gradients use alpha.

**How to apply:** Keep the hero overlay transparent beneath its gradients. Verify that the bottles remain visible when changing theme styles.
